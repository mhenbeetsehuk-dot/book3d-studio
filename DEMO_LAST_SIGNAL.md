# LAST SIGNAL — 30-second Graphic Novel / Motion Comic acceptance film

**Format:** 6 scenes × approximately 5 seconds.  
**Primary visual mode:** original stylized graphic novel / motion comic.  
**Audio:** two offline voices, original procedural ambient score, procedural sound effects.  
**Assembly:** each illustrated panel is animated independently, mixed with audio, then stitched into one 30-second MP4.

## Visual bible
- Heavy black ink outlines.
- Charcoal/black base palette.
- Cyan signal light and technology accents.
- Amber visor for Mara Voss.
- Red alarms for danger scenes.
- Subtle halftone texture.
- Consistent Mara Voss helmet, visor, suit and cyan trim across scenes.

## Story
Salvage pilot Mara Voss enters an abandoned lunar relay and finds one transmitter still alive. A damaged station intelligence warns her not to answer an unknown signal. The relay transmits anyway, and the returning signal speaks in Mara's own voice. When the station seals her inside, Mara tears out the power core. Outside, a colossal structure buried beneath the crater wakes—and whispers, “Thank you.”

## Six panels/scenes
1. **The Dead Relay (0–5s):** Mara enters; one cyan console wakes.
2. **The Warning (5–10s):** Station Voice: “Do not answer the signal.”
3. **It Answers (10–15s):** The signal replies: “Mara... I found you.”
4. **Seal (15–20s):** Hatch locks. Station Voice: “Step away from the console.”
5. **Cut the Power (20–25s):** Mara pulls the core: “Not today.”
6. **Below the Crater (25–30s):** Buried alien geometry lights up. Station Voice: “Thank you.”
